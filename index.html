The Perspective

Direct, lifestyle-fluent, and unpretentious.

I split my time between the high-paced rhythms of Singapore and the grounded complexity of Bangkok. My background isn't in ashrams, but in the reality of building a life that feels as good on the inside as it looks on the outside.

I don't offer shortcuts or transcendental promises. I offer a practice. A way to observe the noise without becoming it. A way to be direct with yourself when everything else is polished.

Whether we meet in a quiet studio in Sukhumvit or via a screen from a high-rise in the CBD, the goal remains: to help you feel like yourself again.

You already have the equipment. We are just learning how to use it.

Stop chasing. Start being.
